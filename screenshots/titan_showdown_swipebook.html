DAY 1 • OPENING ROUND

ELITE LAND THE FIRST BLOW

Ricky Snell fires 29 Stableford points and ELITE win the opening day by five, but the Renegades and Legion Six are already in the fight.

How Day 1 unfolded

ELITE were quickest out of the blocks. Ricky Snell birdied three of his first six holes and never gave the field a chance to settle. Levi Lord added 24 points, with Chris Johnson and Mike Martin combining for 38 more to take ELITE to 91.

The Renegades stayed close all afternoon. Ross Snell's 27 and Kevin Smith's closing run dragged them to 86, while Brad Kiddell's five-birdie round gave Legion Six an 82-point platform. The early warning: this was not going to be a runaway.

DAY 2 • MOVING DAY

RENEGADES HIT BACK AT WEST CLIFFS

The tournament changes colour as Ross Snell's Renegades post the best team score of the week so far and take a four-point lead.

Day 2 story

West Cliffs punished anyone missing fairways, but the Renegades embraced the wind. Ross Snell backed up his opening 27 with 28, Kevin Smith added 25 and Darren Samways produced the surprise round of the day with 23.

Their 95-point team total erased ELITE's five-point advantage. ELITE were solid rather than spectacular on 86, while Legion Six stayed in touch on 81. The MOB's Tony Martin produced 24 points, but his side could not build around him.

DAY 3 • CHAMPIONSHIP SATURDAY

SIX POINTS. THREE TEAMS. ONE ROUND LEFT.

ELITE retake the lead, Legion Six surge, and the Titan Showdown sets up the finish it wanted.

The turning point

ELITE arrived four behind and left three in front. Levi Lord led their charge with 25 points, Chris Johnson found his best golf of the trip for 23, and Ricky Snell ground out 23 despite a double bogey on the 7th.

Legion Six were the day's big climbers. Brad Kiddell and Joe Bond combined for 49, pushing them to within six of the lead. The Renegades slipped to second after their first sub-90 team total.

Final tournament story

ELITE led after Day 1, surrendered top spot to the Renegades on Day 2, reclaimed it by three points on Day 3 and then produced their best team round of the week on the final day.

The winning margin was just five points. Across four rounds Ricky Snell was the tournament's most consistent player, Levi Lord produced the single best round, and Brad Kiddell's 18 birdies kept Legion Six in contention until late on Saturday.
